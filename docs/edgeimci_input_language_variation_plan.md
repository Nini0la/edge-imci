# EdgeIMCI input-language variation plan

> **Authority:** `WORKING_DATASET_PLAN` · **Lifecycle:** `BOUNDED_CANARY_REVIEW_COMPLETE` · Language/dataset design only; no clinical-rule change, bulk-generation authorization or training authorization.

## Purpose and architectural context

EdgeIMCI's primary learned task is:

> **free-form PHC findings → model-facing structured encounter JSON**

The model-facing JSON is validated and passed to the deterministic completeness validator and deterministic IMCI decision engine. The model interprets language; it does not own classifications, urgency, referral or treatment selection.

Input-language variation is therefore a first-class dataset variable. Different faithful linguistic realizations of one parent encounter should map to the same canonical structured target:

```text
Clinical standard English ─┐
Natural English            │
Nigerian English           │
Nigerian Pidgin            │
Non-standard English       ├──> SAME model-facing encounter JSON
Noisy typed English        │
Telegraphic PHC notes      │
Mixed-register input       ┘
```

This is the reverse of the teacher-generation direction:

```text
frozen structured encounter
        ↓
teacher generates varied PHC-worker input
        ↓
mechanical and semantic review
        ↓
accepted input + deterministic model-facing JSON target
```

The teacher generates language and fact-evidence annotations only. It must not generate the structured target. The target remains a deterministic projection of the frozen semantic source under `edge-imci-model-facing-encounter-v1`.

## Language-variant families

The variant family is an experimental label describing the intended surface register. It must never imply a different clinical target.

### `CLINICAL_STANDARD_ENGLISH`

Concise, professional PHC-style English with relatively clean grammar and conventional clinical terminology. This family provides the cleanest extraction baseline and should not become artificially ornate or specialist-heavy.

### `NATURAL_CONVERSATIONAL_ENGLISH`

Connected, less formal prose that remains clear and clinically faithful. The currently approved natural-English extraction canaries belong closest to this family and should be preserved as the initial reference.

### `NIGERIAN_ENGLISH`

Recognizably Nigerian English phrasing, cadence and usage while remaining English. This family must not be reduced to spelling mistakes, exaggerated expressions or caricature. Its prompt and review should focus on genuine local usage and semantic precision.

### `NIGERIAN_PIDGIN`

Genuine Nigerian Pidgin constructions used as a distinct controlled generation strategy. Pidgin is not corrupted English and must not be generated by mechanically damaging English text. Every source observation, explicit negative, logical connector, measurement, duration, qualifier and UNKNOWN distinction must survive the realization.

### `NONSTANDARD_ENGLISH`

English containing controlled grammatical irregularities, such as article omission, subject/verb disagreement, tense inconsistency, fragments or unconventional sentence structure. Use this neutral label rather than terms such as “deformed English.” The irregularity must affect surface form only.

### `NOISY_TYPED_ENGLISH`

Input reflecting realistic typing noise, including spelling mistakes, punctuation loss, casing variation, common abbreviations and fragmented entry. Noise must be bounded and recorded so the text remains clinically interpretable and auditable.

### `TELEGRAPHIC_PHC_NOTE`

Compact note-style input, for example:

```text
18mo cough 3d, RR45 calm, chest indrawing +
```

Compression is allowed only where the notation remains unambiguous under the approved source facts. A short note is not permission to omit required known observations from a complete parent encounter.

### `MIXED_REGISTER`

Mostly English with controlled colloquial or local phrasing and occasional Pidgin-like constructions. The result must remain interpretable, internally coherent and faithful to one source encounter. This family should not become an untraceable catch-all for arbitrary variation.

## Semantic invariants

Variation is permitted only in surface form. Every accepted variant must preserve:

- every known-positive observation;
- every explicit negative;
- every measurement and its unit or encoded interpretation;
- every duration;
- every clinically relevant qualifier;
- logical connectors, especially `or` versus `and`;
- epistemic qualifiers such as `identified`, `known`, `confirmed`, `observed`, `reported` or `history of`;
- measurement and assessment-validity conditions where represented;
- pre-intervention and post-intervention state;
- every UNKNOWN value as unknown rather than absent;
- pathway-entry and scope/context facts required by the structured target; and
- distinctions between related sources of evidence where the current substrate represents them, such as reported ear discharge versus pus observed during examination.

An accepted variant must not:

- add or omit a clinical fact;
- strengthen or weaken a clinical claim;
- convert UNKNOWN into absent or negative;
- convert `or` into `and`;
- convert `no identified X` into `no X exists`;
- merge two distinct observations into one unsupported claim;
- invent shorthand whose meaning is ambiguous or different;
- imply a classification, urgency state, referral decision or treatment;
- leak rule IDs, evaluator traces, field paths, schema names or target-side terminology; or
- use expected downstream results to make the PHC submission unnaturally diagnostic.

The recently observed changes from “able to drink or breastfeed” to “able to drink and breastfeed,” and from “no identified bacterial cause” to “no bacterial cause is present,” are semantic failures under every variant family.

## Controlled noise profiles

Non-standard or noisy language should be generated from explicit profiles rather than a vague instruction to “make the English bad.” Supported experimental dimensions may include:

- `ARTICLE_OMISSION`;
- `SUBJECT_VERB_DISAGREEMENT`;
- `TENSE_INCONSISTENCY`;
- `PUNCTUATION_LOSS`;
- `CASING_VARIATION`;
- `SENTENCE_FRAGMENTS`;
- `SPELLING_NOISE`;
- `ABBREVIATION_DENSITY_LOW`;
- `ABBREVIATION_DENSITY_MEDIUM`; and
- `TELEGRAPHIC_COMPRESSION`.

Profiles should be kept small and interpretable during canary work. Combining many transformations at once makes semantic failures harder to attribute. A conceptual provenance fragment is:

```json
{
  "variant_style": "NOISY_TYPED_ENGLISH",
  "noise_profile": [
    "PUNCTUATION_LOSS",
    "ARTICLE_OMISSION",
    "SPELLING_NOISE"
  ]
}
```

These fields are now versioned in `edge-imci-structured-extraction-sft-record-v1` as `language_provenance.variant_style` and `language_provenance.noise_profile`. Existing approved natural-English samples were rebuilt with provenance without regenerating their language.

## Pidgin-specific safeguards

`NIGERIAN_PIDGIN` requires:

- its own pinned prompt strategy;
- a small target-blind canary before any larger run;
- semantic comparison against the frozen structured source;
- explicit review of negation and logical connectors;
- explicit review of measurements, durations and pre/post-intervention wording;
- explicit review of epistemic qualifiers and reported-versus-observed evidence; and
- review by a competent Nigerian Pidgin speaker before the strategy is described as reliable.

The bounded three-case Pidgin canary passed the delegated hackathon-suitability review. This permits further controlled generation under a separately authorized run; it is not expert linguistic certification, bulk-generation authorization or a claim that every Nigerian Pidgin register is covered.

## Provenance and split inheritance

Each variant should retain, directly or through its parent run and canonical record, enough information to support stratified analysis:

- `variant_style`;
- `noise_profile`, when applicable;
- `source_case_id`;
- inherited parent-semantic partition;
- `generation_run_id` and attempt identity;
- teacher provider, model and immutable snapshot;
- prompt ID, version and hash;
- target-schema ID and hash;
- dataset-policy and split-policy IDs;
- deterministic validation result and error codes; and
- semantic/naturalness/PHC-suitability review status.

Do not duplicate data already pinned by an immutable run or manifest merely to make each row larger. The canonical record should contain stable identifiers and hashes that resolve the evidence.

Under `edge-imci-parent-semantic-split-v1`, the split unit is the parent semantic encounter. Every language realization of one encounter inherits the same `TRAIN`, `VALIDATION` or `TEST` partition. Language family, teacher, prompt, noise profile and variant ID must never be used to move a paraphrase into another partition.

## Scientific questions and evaluation

The goal is not merely to obtain “more diverse data.” The scientific question is:

> How robust is EdgeIMCI structured extraction as PHC input moves from clean clinical English toward natural Nigerian English, Nigerian Pidgin, telegraphic notes and noisy or non-standard English?

Report extraction metrics by language family and, where sample sizes permit, by noise profile:

- schema-valid output rate;
- whole-record exact match;
- field-level accuracy;
- known-positive precision and recall;
- known-negative accuracy;
- UNKNOWN-preservation accuracy;
- measurement and duration accuracy;
- qualifier preservation;
- pre/post-intervention state accuracy;
- downstream deterministic decision equivalence; and
- separately visible urgent-action equivalence.

Aggregate scores must not hide a severe failure concentrated in one language family. Evaluation sets should contain parent encounters distinct from training parents, and every style comparison should identify its case coverage and sample count.

## Current authorization boundary

The project owner separately authorized exactly 12 Azure GPT-4.1 canary request starts under a $1 cap and delegated hackathon-scoped Pidgin review. That bounded run is complete with zero retries: 9 candidates were approved and 3 rejected. Its evidence and review are recorded under `experiments/generation/holistic-input-style-canary-gpt41-20250414-v1/`, `docs/input_language_style_canary_review_v1.md`, and `data/canary/input_language_styles_v1/`.

The first canary did not authorize further calls. The project owner subsequently authorized a separate six-attempt, $0.50-capped remediation canary. It completed with zero retries and a $0.18 conservative reservation: noisy typed English v1.1 passed 3/3, while Nigerian English v1.1 passed 2/3 because one response shifted area-level malaria risk onto the child. Five new language-to-JSON records were approved.

The project owner then authorized a three-attempt Nigerian English v1.2 validation canary with a $0.25 ceiling. It completed with zero retries, 4,616 input tokens, 2,364 output tokens and a $0.09 conservative reservation. Two candidates passed at generation time. The third language submission was clinically and semantically faithful but had three non-literal evidence-span annotations; its immutable rejection was preserved, and a separate review artifact corrected only the annotations. All three variants passed revalidation and delegated semantic review. Nigerian English v1.2 is therefore approved for further controlled generation.

The project owner then authorized a 24-parent Nigerian English v1.2 pathway pilot under a $1 ceiling. It completed with 24 starts, zero retries, 37,164 input tokens, 20,133 output tokens and a $0.72 conservative reservation. The original gate passed 23/24; strengthened semantic validation and delegated review approved 19/24. One additional response was annotation-only and was retained after an auditable evidence-span correction. Five semantic variants were rejected: one collapsed diarrhoea-specific normal drinking status into the weaker general ability to drink, and four weakened a known-negative ear-discharge history into absence of reporting.

No additional remote calls, bulk generation, training, or production clinical use are now authorized. Further controlled generation may use Nigerian Pidgin v1, telegraphic PHC note v1 and noisy typed English v1.1 under a new run authorization. Nigerian English should use the prepared v1.3 prompt only after a new bounded validation confirms the two remediations.
