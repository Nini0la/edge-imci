# EdgeIMCI Nigerian English v1.2 pathway pilot

> Authority: PROJECT_OWNER_AUTHORIZED_CONTROLLED_PILOT · Lifecycle: REVIEW_COMPLETE

Twenty-four target-blind GPT-4.1 attempts covered distinct eligible parent encounters across the supported pathways and decision states. The run completed with 24 starts and zero retries. Strengthened validation and delegated semantic review approved 19 variants and rejected five.

One rejected-at-generation fever response required annotation-only evidence-span remediation and was approved without changing its language. The five semantic rejections remain immutable evidence: one conflated general ability to drink with diarrhoea-specific normal drinking status, and four weakened a known-negative ear-discharge history into absence of reporting.

Nigerian English v1.2 is marked REVISE_BEFORE_SCALE. Prompt v1.3 is prepared but unvalidated. No additional calls, bulk generation, training, or production clinical use are authorized.
